## About this book {#about-this-book}

Humans are social animals whose behaviour is steered by a handful of chemicals, and leaders — in any group, with or without rank — are the people who create or destroy the conditions in which those chemicals reward cooperation. Sinek's central image is the **Circle of Safety**: a perimeter of trust that leaders build by putting the people in their care before themselves ("leaders eat last"). When people feel safe inside it they cooperate and look out for each other; when they don't, [cortisol]({{< relref "../../topics/cortisol.md" >}}) makes everyone guard themselves.

The wiki ingest follows two threads: the chemicals themselves, and what leadership means outside management and work — parenting, friendship, adolescence, addiction recovery and shared struggle.


## Key concepts {#key-concepts}

-   [Leadership]({{< relref "../../topics/leadership.md" >}}) — leadership as a choice to look after the people in your care; the cost of leadership; leadership as parenting; service as the way out of addiction
-   [Dopamine]({{< relref "../../topics/dopamine.md" >}}) — chemical of progress and instant gratification; tangible goals; addictions to alcohol, gambling, phones and social media
-   [Endorphins]({{< relref "../../topics/endorphins.md" >}}) — pain-masking and endurance; runner's high; laughter
-   [Serotonin]({{< relref "../../topics/serotonin.md" >}}) — pride, status and responsibility towards those we lead or are led by
-   [Oxytocin]({{< relref "../../topics/oxytocin.md" >}}) — trust, love and belonging; touch and generosity; its role in stress, immunity, addiction recovery and shared hardship
-   [Cortisol]({{< relref "../../topics/cortisol.md" >}}) — stress and the early-warning system; health costs; work-life balance as "where we feel safe"
-   [Psychological safety]({{< relref "../../topics/psychological_safety.md" >}}) — the team-level counterpart of the Circle of Safety
-   [Attention economy]({{< relref "../../topics/attention_economy.md" >}}) — notifications and likes as dopamine triggers
