** Description
Simon Sinek (2014) argues that humans are social animals whose behaviour is steered by a handful of chemicals — endorphins, dopamine, serotonin, oxytocin and cortisol — and that leaders, in any group, are the people who create (or destroy) the conditions in which those chemicals reward cooperation. His central image is the *Circle of Safety*: a perimeter of trust that leaders build by taking responsibility for the people in their care ("leaders eat last").
